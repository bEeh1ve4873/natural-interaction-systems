# The grand overview
From the very beginning of robotics development, works have been attempting to create a naturalistic human-robot interaction. Publications on the subject could be found every single year (example, Dautenhahn,(1995); Nicolescu,(2003); Leite,(2012);Gkournelos,(2024)). In recent years, more and more publications were reviews on specific topics within this big arena.  That begs the question of whether development has stalled and why is that. 

## Leveraging LLM
Recent reviews have reached an agreement on the power of incorporating LLM into robotic systems for interaction. These reviews provide the "how" into harnessing capabilities of LLM into robotics (Wang, 2025). In particular, the main capability of LLM that is being stressed is the ability to process natural language proficiently and to reason accordingly  (Wang, 2025). The advantage of LLM is to understnad even vague textual instruction quite well. That, in turn, significantly enhance robots’ comprehension of instructions, environments, and required actions (Wang, 2025).
