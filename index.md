This repository is my working notebook on making human–robot interaction feel natural. My focus is ergonomics: not just whether a robot is safe or usable, but whether interaction with it feels effortless, intuitive, and physically and cognitively sustainable for the people involved.

I am a systems builder and reviewer working at the intersection of robotics, ergonomics, and naturalistic interaction. My background includes building systems, applying AI, and publishing in academic journals. This repo collects my responses to literature I read, short reviews of specific topics, and notes toward a larger long-term vision: an ecosystem of robotic systems that coordinate and interact in reality in a naturalistic way.

If you are working on ergonomics, naturalistic HRI, or multi-robot coordination, the notes here may be useful, and I welcome correspondence.
